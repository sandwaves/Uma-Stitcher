How to use

1. Take your screenshots
・Scroll through the screen and take screenshots as you go.
・Each screenshot should overlap the next by at least two rows.
・Make sure nothing outside the game window is captured (notifications, taskbars, etc).
・Most scrollable screens are supported, including factor / skill / inheritance / training tabs, finish-order tables, score info, entry lists, and support card lists.

2. Load the screenshots
・Drag and drop, choose files, or paste from the clipboard (Ctrl+V).
・Drag the previews to reorder them; click one to see it full-size.
・You can load multiple characters at once.
・Tap the ✕ on a preview to remove it.

3. Tap Generate
・Takes anywhere from instant to a few seconds depending on how many images you loaded.

4. Save the result
・Tap the output image to switch between full size and fit-to-width.
・Use the JPG / PNG / 📋 buttons to save.
・On iOS, long-press the image to save it to your photo library.
・Reset clears everything so you can start over.

Optional: skill icons (β)
・Adds a small skill icon next to each factor.
・Stat-boosting factors (e.g. scenario factors) show a blue icon.
・If the factor name can't be read clearly, [?] shows instead.
・Higher-resolution screenshots give better results — at least 1500px tall is recommended.
・Skill data provided by U-tools.
