How to use

Taking screenshots

  • Scroll through the list and take a screenshot each time, overlapping the previous one by at least two rows.
  • Take them all the same way: same device, same window size, and no cropping.
  • Keep notifications, the taskbar and anything outside the game window out of the shot.
  • Most scrolling screens work, such as the Skills, Inspiration and Career Info tabs, race results, entry lists and support card lists.

Stitching and saving

  • Screenshots are matched by where they overlap, so their order usually doesn't matter. If some can't be matched, they're stacked in the numbered order. Use ‹ › (or drag on desktop) to change it.
  • You can add screenshots of several characters at once. Each one gets its own column.
  • On iPhone or iPad, long-press the result to save it to Photos.

Skill icons (beta)

  • Adds the matching skill icon next to each skill spark.
  • Sparks without a skill icon, such as race and scenario sparks, get a [?]. So do names that can't be read.
  • Screenshots at least 1500px tall give the best results.
  • Skill data from U-tools.
